Why These Platforms Matter
📊Aladdin — The Investment OS
Aladdin is unique because it runs the same risk models that BlackRock uses internally. When $21T in assets across 200+ institutions use the same platform, it creates network effects — shared benchmarks, consistent risk language and standardised data that individual firms can't replicate alone.
🏛️Axiom SL — The Reg Compliance Engine
Regulatory reporting is one of the highest-cost, highest-risk activities in banking. Axiom SL reduces that risk by maintaining pre-built regulatory content for 50+ frameworks across 30+ jurisdictions — removing the need for banks to build and maintain custom calculation engines in-house.
⚡Murex — The Derivatives Workhorse
MX.3 is the dominant platform for OTC derivatives at tier-1 banks because it handles the full lifecycle — from exotic pricing models (Monte Carlo, PDE) through real-time XVA to CCP clearing and EMIR reporting — on a single codebase. Replacing it typically takes 5–10 years.
🏗️Calypso — The CCP & Treasury Specialist
Calypso carved a niche by being the only major vendor with deep CCP technology. Multiple central counterparties run their clearing and default management on Calypso. Its collateral optimisation engine is also best-in-class for banks managing large bilateral and cleared portfolios.
💼Triple'A — The Private Bank Platform
Wealth management demands a different system than capital markets — client-first reporting, mandate compliance, GIPS performance and advisory suitability matter more than raw trading throughput. Triple'A built specifically for private banks, giving it depth in client-centric features that capital-markets platforms lack.
